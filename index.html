01

About us

Founded in 2018 in Pasir Mas, Kelantan, SH Global Solution builds fibre-optic and civil infrastructure for telecom operators, developers, and governments across Malaysia.

Our mission is to design, build, and maintain advanced communication networks that empower communities, businesses, and governments — on time, within budget, and to the highest quality standards.

02

What we do

End-to-end fibre and civil works — from network design and cable laying to splicing, testing, and long-term maintenance — built for performance with minimal downtime.

FTTH (Fiber to the Home) Projects

We offer complete FTTH solutions, including network design, fiber laying, splicing, termination, and testing. Our projects ensure high-speed internet connectivity for residential, commercial, and institutional clients.

OSP & ODN Projects

Our outside plant and optical distribution network services include trenching, ducting, cable pulling, joint closures, and connectivity to end users. We provide end-to-end support from site survey to final commissioning.

CB & Titan Projects

We deliver high-capacity network solutions involving cable broadband and Titan infrastructure, integrating copper and fiber-based systems to serve high-density urban and suburban areas.

Network Maintenance Services

We provide comprehensive network maintenance programs, including preventive inspections, emergency repairs, service level agreements (SLAs), and system upgrades to ensure 24/7 reliability.

Civil Engineering Works

Our civil work division handles the physical infrastructure, such as duct installations, pole erection, chamber construction, manholes, and concrete structures required for telecom networks.

Fiber Termination & Splicing

We carry out precise fiber optic splicing and termination using high-accuracy tools and certified technicians, ensuring seamless data transmission with minimal loss and maximum performance.
